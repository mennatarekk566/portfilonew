01 — ABOUT ME

My story is about trying, building, failing, and trying again.

I didn’t start with everything figured out. I started as a Computer Engineering student who was curious about technology and wanted to understand what happens behind the screen.

At first, I was learning the basics and writing small pieces of code. Sometimes the code worked, sometimes it didn’t, and sometimes one missing line was enough to make me spend hours looking for the problem. Instead of stopping there, I kept trying, searching, changing things, and learning from every mistake.

With time, I started building more. I moved from simple programs to web development, then deeper into C#, .NET, databases, APIs, authentication, and backend architecture. Every new project became a chance to take what I had learned and actually use it.

I also learned that becoming a developer is not only about knowing a programming language. It is about solving problems, understanding what people need, breaking a big problem into smaller parts, and having the patience to keep working until the solution makes sense.

I’m still on that journey. I keep learning, I keep building, and I keep trying to turn ideas into working products. My goal is not to know everything — it is to keep improving and become better with every project I create.
